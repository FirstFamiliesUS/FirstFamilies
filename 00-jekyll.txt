 JekyllThemes.io
This is the most curated and popular directory specifically for Jekyll themes. They have a mix of free and premium options, usually ranging from $49 to $99.

Categories: They offer highly tailored themes for Blogs, Portfolios, Business/SaaS landing pages, and Documentation.

Benefits: Themes here are explicitly built with GitHub Pages compatibility in mind and are usually well-documented and optimized.

2. ThemeForest (Envato Market)
ThemeForest is the largest marketplace for website templates in general, and they have a dedicated "Jamstack" section that includes hundreds of Jekyll themes.

Price Range: Usually $19 to $79.

Benefits: You can find highly complex, multi-purpose themes here. If you need a site with many different types of pages, interactive UI elements, or specialized integrations, ThemeForest has the largest variety.


3. Independent Theme Creators
Many developers sell high-quality boutique Jekyll themes directly from their own websites. A notable example is Hydejack, a popular boutique theme designed specifically for academics, hackers, and portfolio sites, which offers both a free version and a paid PRO version with advanced features.
