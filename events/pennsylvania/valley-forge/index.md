# Valley Forge, Pennsylvania

Valley Forge holds a monumental place in American history as the site of the Continental Army's winter encampment from December 19, 1777 to June 19, 1778. Under the leadership of General George Washington, the Continental troops endured severe hardships, transformed into a disciplined military force, and solidified their path toward American independence.

---

## Lineage & Historical Societies

### Society of the Descendants of Washington's Army at Valley Forge

<img src="{{ '/events/pennsylvania/valley-forge/media/media/image1.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Society of the Descendants of Washington's Army at Valley Forge Insignia">

A national lineage, historical, and genealogical organization dedicated to preserving the memory and sacrifices of those who served under General George Washington during the critical six-month encampment at Valley Forge.

* **Membership Eligibility:** Direct lineage must be proven from an officer, soldier, or civilian who served or provided documented support to the Continental Army at Valley Forge between **December 19, 1777 and June 19, 1778**.
* **Society Information & Application:** [Descendants of Washington's Army at Valley Forge Guide (PDF)](https://www.valleyforgesociety.com/_files/ugd/cbe2c2_72d505a5537b47a981e600f0195a743e.pdf)
