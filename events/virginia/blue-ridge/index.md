## Historic Churches & Landmarks

### Bedford Baptist Church

Organized in 1797 and located at the foot of the scenic Peaks of Otter in Bedford, Virginia, Bedford Baptist Church is steeped in over two centuries of local historical tradition.

* **Address:** 1516 Oakwood Street, Bedford, VA 24523
* **Website:** [Bedford Baptist Church Info](https://sites.google.com/bedfordbaptist.org/bedfordbaptistchurch/who-we-are)

#### Historical Lineage
* **1797:** Organized under the name **Little Otter Baptist Church**
* **1854:** Renamed to **Liberty Baptist Church**
* **1901:** Renamed to **Bedford City Baptist Church**
* **1923:** Renamed to **Bedford Baptist Church**
