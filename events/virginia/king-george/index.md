# King George, Virginia

Situated in the historic Northern Neck peninsula between the Potomac and Rappahannock rivers, King George County features rich colonial heritage, historic parishes, and vibrant community traditions.

---

## Historic Churches & Events

### St. Paul's Episcopal Church

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/king-george/media/media/image1.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="St. Paul's Episcopal Church">

Historic colonial parish serving King George County since the 18th century, hosting community events and celebrations throughout the year.

* **Address:** [5486 St. Pauls Road, King George, VA 22485](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=5486+St.+Pauls+Road+King+George+VA+22485)
* **Phone:** [(540) 663-3085](tel:+15406633085)
* **Website:** [stpaulskgva.org](https://www.stpaulskgva.org/)

#### Annual Oktoberfest
Gather under the festhaus tent for authentic food, drink, and activities, including live music from a German oompah band. Features a local **Christkindlmarkt** with artisan crafts and family-friendly activities for all ages.

* **Event Details:** [St. Paul's Oktoberfest Info](https://www.stpaulskgva.org/oktoberfest/)
