# Northumberland, Virginia

Formed in 1648, Northumberland County is one of the oldest political subdivisions in Virginia, sitting on the easternmost tip of the Northern Neck along the Chesapeake Bay. It features deep roots in early colonial parish history, historic Lee family plantations, and rich coastal wildlife preserves.

## Historic Parishes & Churches

### St. Stephen's Episcopal Church

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/northumberland/media/media/image1.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="St. Stephen's Episcopal Church">

Formed in 1698 by combining Bowtracy and Fairfield (Chickacoan) parishes. St. Stephen's was represented at the first convention of the Episcopal Church of Virginia in 1785.

## Historic Plantations & Markers

### Ditchley Plantation

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/northumberland/media/media/image7.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Ditchley Plantation">

Land patented in 1651 by Col. Richard Lee I and named for a Lee estate near Oxford, England. The original house dated to 1687, while the current two-story Georgian brick mansion was built in 1762 by Kendall Lee.

* **Coordinates:** [37°44′03″N 76°20′05″W](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Northumberland_County,_Virginia&params=37.734167_N_76.334722_W_&title=Ditchley)
* **Designation:** NRHP [#92001272](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/92001272)
* **Reference:** [Ditchley Wikipedia Entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ditchley_%28Kilmarnock,_Virginia%29)

---

### Cobbs Hall

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/northumberland/media/media/image8.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Cobbs Hall Plantation">

Acquired by Richard Lee prior to 1651, with an early structure built by Charles Lee in 1720. The current double-pile brick mansion was constructed in 1853 and includes the historic Cobbs Hall Lee family graveyard.

* **Address:** 582 Cobbs Hall Ln., Kilmarnock, VA
* **Designation:** NRHP [#01000699](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/01000699)
* **Social:** [Cobbs Hall Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100064819893078)

## Nature Preserves & Cruises

### Dameron Marsh Natural Area Preserve

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/northumberland/media/media/image5.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Dameron Marsh Natural Area Preserve">

A 316-acre preserve supporting salt marsh communities, pristine sand beaches, and upland forest habitats. Critical habitat for marsh-bird communities and the federally threatened northeastern beach tiger beetle.

* **Location:** Near Kilmarnock, VA *(Plus Code: QMMX+3C)*
* **Preserve Details:** [DCR Dameron Marsh Preserve Page](https://www.dcr.virginia.gov/natural-heritage/natural-area-preserves/dameron)
