# Virginia Beach & Historic Hampton Roads

Whether visiting the coastal resort area of Virginia Beach or exploring the historic roots of early American settlement nearby, the lower Tidewater and Historic Triangle regions offer a ideal blend of historical exploration and oceanfront recreation.

---

## Virginia Beach Oceanfront & Hospitality

### Hilton Virginia Beach Oceanfront

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image1.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Hilton Virginia Beach Oceanfront">

Located directly along the boardwalk at 31st Street, offering panoramic ocean views and prime access to live outdoor entertainment next door at Neptune’s Park.

## Virginia Beach Attractions & Family Fun

### First Landing / First Cross

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image5.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="First Cross at Seashore State Park">

Commemorating the initial 1607 landing site of the English colonists before moving inland up the James River.

* **Designations:** Seashore State Park Historic District, U.S. National Register of Historic Places, Virginia Landmarks Register.

## Historic Triangle Excursions

Connected via the scenic **Colonial Parkway** (<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image8.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="Colonial Parkway" style="display:inline; max-height:1.2em;">), linking Jamestown, Williamsburg, and Yorktown.

---

### Jamestown & Early Settlement

#### Historic Jamestowne

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image3.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Jamestowne National Park">

The original site of the first permanent English settlement in North America (1607). Features active archaeological digs at James Fort, living history, and the historic Glasshouse.

* **Website:** [Jamestowne National Historic Site (NPS)](https://www.nps.gov/jame/index.htm)

---

#### Jamestowne Society

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image17.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Jamestowne Society">

A lineage society preserving the heritage of descendants whose ancestors resided or held government roles in Jamestowne prior to 1700.

* **Genealogy & Lineage:** [Jamestowne Society Lineage Project](https://www.jamestowne.org/lineage-paper-project.html)

---

#### Jamestown-Scotland Ferry

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image19.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Jamestown Ferry">

A **FREE** 24/7 automobile ferry crossing the James River between Route 31 at Jamestown and Scotland Wharf in Surry County.

* **Details:** [VDOT Jamestown Ferry Information](https://www.vdot.virginia.gov/travel/ferry-jamestown.asp)

---

### Colonial Williamsburg

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image9.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Colonial Williamsburg">

A 301-acre living-history museum depicting Virginia’s 18th-century capital with restored historic buildings, artisan shops, and costumed trade interpreters.

* **House of Burgesses:** (<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image6.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="House of Burgesses" style="display:inline; max-height:1.2em;">) Read more about the elected legislative body of colonial Virginia ([Wikipedia Entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Burgesses)).
* **Bruton Parish Church:** (<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image7.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="Bruton Parish Church" style="display:inline; max-height:1.2em;">) Historic 18th-century Episcopal parish where George Washington, Thomas Jefferson, and James Madison worshipped ([Bruton Parish Heritage](https://www.brutonparish.org/heritage)).
* **Busch Gardens Williamsburg:** (<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image4.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="Busch Gardens Williamsburg" style="display:inline; max-height:1.2em;">) Nearby European-themed amusement park.

---

### Yorktown Battlefield & Museums

#### Yorktown Battlefield & Visitor Center

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image10.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Yorktown Visitor Center">

Site of the pivotal 1781 Revolutionary War siege where Washington and French allies secured Lord Cornwallis's surrender.

* **Battlefield Details:** [Yorktown National Battlefield (NPS)](https://www.nps.gov/york/index.htm)
* **Junior Ranger Program:** (<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image18.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="Yorktown Junior Ranger" style="display:inline; max-height:1.2em;">) Self-guided 2-hour program for kids up to age 12 ([Yorktown Junior Ranger Info](https://www.nps.gov/york/learn/kidsyouth/beajuniorranger.htm)).

---

#### American Revolution Museum at Yorktown

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/virginia-beach/media/media/image20.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="American Revolution Museum at Yorktown">

An immersive museum experience detailing the founding of the nation through gallery exhibits, a re-created Continental Army encampment, and a working 18th-century farm.

* **Events & Admission:** [American Revolution Museum at Yorktown](https://www.jyfmuseums.org/events)
