# Richmond, Virginia

The Greater Richmond region serves as a central historic hub for Virginia's early colonial past, including key sites along the historic Huguenot Trail where early French Protestant refugees established their community in the early 18th century.

* **Driving Tour:** [Richmond Driving Tour Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BJ1p5Qyq6shU7e4p8)

---

## Historic Societies & Research

### Huguenot Society of the Founders of Manakin

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/richmond/media/media/image1.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Huguenot Society of the Founders of Manakin">

Dedicated to preserving the history and lineage of all French Protestant Huguenots who arrived in Virginia prior to 1786. The Society maintains a national headquarters and a specialized genealogical research library.

* **Address:** [981 Huguenot Trail, Midlothian, VA 23113](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=981+Huguenot+Trail+Midlothian+VA+23113)
* **Genealogy Resources:** [Registered Huguenot Lineages](https://www.huguenotmanakin.org/copy-of-old-registered-lineages)

## Historic Parishes & Monuments

### Manakin Episcopal Church

<img src="{{ '/events/virginia/richmond/media/media/image2.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Manakin Episcopal Church">

Tracing its origins to 1701, the founding community of French Huguenots fled religious persecution following Louis XIV's revocation of the Edict of Nantes in 1685. With financial aid from King William III and land provided along the James River, they established King William Parish.

* **Address:** [985 Huguenot Trail, Midlothian, VA 23113](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=985+Huguenot+Trail+Midlothian+VA+23113)
* **Phone:** [(804) 794-6401](tel:+18047946401)
* **History:** [Manakin Episcopal Church History](https://manakin.org/history/)
