## President's Park & National Christmas Tree

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/PresidentsPark.jpg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="President's Park">

### [President's Park](https://www.nps.gov/whho/planyourvisit/explore-president-s-park.htm)

The sidewalks through President's Park allow you to explore the grounds at your leisure. To help you locate and learn about the monuments, the park is split into two primary self-guided walking tours:

* **[Northern Trail through Lafayette Park](https://www.nps.gov/whho/planyourvisit/explore-the-northern-trail.htm):** Begins at the White House Visitor Center, passes Sherman Park—where General Sherman reviewed Civil War troops—continues north through Lafayette Park, and concludes at the First Division Monument.
* **[Southern Trail through the Ellipse](https://www.nps.gov/whho/planyourvisit/explore-the-southern-trail.htm):** Winds around the large open parade ground of the Ellipse, past monuments, memorials, and fountains, ending near the White House South Lawn fence.

## St. John’s, Lafayette Square

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/stJohnsEpiscopal.jpg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="St. John's Episcopal Church">

### [St. John’s Church, Lafayette Square](https://stjohns-dc.org/about/calendar/)

Beginning with James Madison, every U.S. President has attended a service at St. John’s, earning it the title "the Church of the Presidents." Designated a National Historic Landmark, Pew 54 is designated as the President's Pew.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/LafayetteSquare.jpg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Lafayette Square View">

* **Service:** 11:00 AM Choral Eucharist
* **Tours:** Guided tours of St. John’s Church and Parish House occur on Sundays following the 11:00 AM service (free of charge).
* **Location:** 16th and H Streets NW, Washington, DC 20005
* **Phone:** [(202) 347-8766](tel:+12023478766)

### Willard History & Museum

## Parking

Free parking is available across parts of Washington D.C. for visitors who know where to look:

1. **Sunday Parking:** Most metered parking spaces in D.C. are free on Sundays.
2. **Residential & Loading Zones:** Check area signage for specified free windows.
3. **Interactive Maps:** Check the [SpotAngels Free Parking Map](https://www.spotangels.com/map?spLng=-77.008173&spLat=38.888995&spAddress=House%20Visitors%20Center,%20Capitol%20Visitors%20Center,%20Washington,%20District%20of%20Columbia%2020024,%20United%20States&lng=-77.008173&lat=38.888995&zoom=15) or reserve a space ahead of time via [SpotHero](https://spothero.com/search?kind=address&latitude=38.895211&longitude=-77.0327373&search_string=White+House+Visitor+Center%2C+Pennsylvania+Avenue+Northwest%2C+Washington%2C+DC%2C+USA).
