# Washington National Cathedral & Regional Attractions

Welcome to the Washington National Cathedral section of First Families. Discover details on cathedral services, special events, dining options, and surrounding points of interest in Washington, DC.

---

## Cathedral Information & Services

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/NationalCathedral.jpg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="National Cathedral">

### [The National Cathedral](https://cathedral.org/calendar/)

The Cathedral administers the Sacrament of [Holy Baptism](https://cathedral.org/worship/baptisms-weddings-funerals/holy-baptism/) on the days recommended in the Book of Common Prayer: All Saints’ Day in the fall, the Feast of the Baptism of our Lord in January, at the Great Vigil of Easter in the spring, and the Day of Pentecost in the early summer. In addition, baptism is offered on the Last Sunday after the Epiphany and the second Sunday in July.

* **Address:** 3101 Wisconsin Ave. NW, Washington, DC 20016
* **Phone:** [(202) 537-6200](tel:+12025376200)
* **Email:** [info@cathedral.org](mailto:info@cathedral.org)

---

### [All Hallows Guild](https://allhallowsguild.org/events/)

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/allHallowsGuild.jpg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="All Hallows Guild">

All Hallows Guild was founded in 1916 to provide for the care and beautification of the grounds of Washington National Cathedral. The Guild works closely with the horticultural staff to preserve this historic landscape and raises funds for renovation and renewal of the gardens.

## Cathedral Events

### [Kirkin’ O’ The Tartan](https://saintandrewsociety.org/events/kirkin/)

The St. Andrew’s Society conducts the Kirkin’ Service at the National Cathedral. Reserved seating in the National Cathedral is available for this service in reserved areas. Reservations are free to all Members and Guests and may be obtained from the Society’s Sergeant-at-Arms and Kirkin’ Chairman David S. McKenzie ([caberman162@yahoo.com](mailto:caberman162@yahoo.com)).

**Schedule of Events:**

* **3:30 PM:** All guests must take their seats in either the general congregation area or in reserved seats in the Front Nave or Great Choir.
* **3:45 PM:** Society Members (preferably in kilts) line up behind the Color Guard and Pipes & Drums for the Procession.
* **4:00 PM:** Procession and service commence.
* **5:00 PM:** Pipes & Drums and Scottish Highland Dancing concert.
* **6:00 PM:** Dinner with the Society.

## Dining Nearby

### Open City Cafe

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/openCityCafe.jpg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Open City Cafe">

Open City at the National Cathedral is a café where locals, visitors, and various communities gather to share a table and enjoy thoughtfully crafted fare. Linger over a latte, have a quiet lunch, or enjoy a brunch date with friends under the backdrop of the National Cathedral. Offering espresso drinks in the Northern Italian tradition, homemade pastries, and sandwiches served on handcrafted rustic breads.

* **Menu:** [View Open City Cathedral Menu](https://www.opencitycathedraldc.com/)

---

### [Duke's Counter](https://www.dukesgrocery.com/dukescounter)

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/dukesCounter.jpg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Duke's Counter">

Located directly across from the main entrance to the National Zoo, this trim, white-walled gastropub welcomes locals, families, and tourists alike with hearty portions, friendly service, a Kids Menu, plus convenient delivery and carryout options. Enjoy eight draught beers, fine wines, and seasonal cocktails from the copper-top bar or relax on the sun-splashed patio.
