# Baltimore, Maryland

Established in 1729 along the Patapsco River, Baltimore is Maryland's largest city and a historic seaport integral to American military, maritime, and religious history. Renowned as the birthplace of the U.S. National Anthem during the War of 1812, Baltimore features rich historic districts, waterfront promenades, and cultural landmarks spanning over three centuries of heritage.

---

## Military, Maritime & National Heritage Sites

### Fort McHenry National Monument & Historic Shrine

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image1.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Fort McHenry National Monument and Historic Shrine">

Famed for its successful defense of Baltimore Harbor during the Battle of Baltimore in September 1814, Fort McHenry directly inspired Francis Scott Key to write the words to "The Star-Spangled Banner."

* **Address:** [2400 East Fort Avenue, Baltimore, MD 21230](https://maps.app.goo.gl/FortMcHenry)
* **Flag Changing Ceremonies:** Conducted every Saturday morning at 10:00 a.m.
* **Information & Schedule:** [Fort McHenry Visitor Calendar](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/calendar.htm)

---

### Fort McHenry Visitor & Education Center

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image4.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Fort McHenry Visitor and Education Center">

Located near the primary entrance lot, the Visitor Center offers orientation films, historical exhibits, and ticket sales for admission into the historic Star Fort.

* **Hours:** 
  * **Regular Hours:** 9:00 a.m. – 4:45 p.m.
  * **Summer Hours (Memorial Day Weekend – Labor Day):** 9:00 a.m. – 5:45 p.m.
* **Admission:** Visitor Center exhibits are free; Star Fort admission tickets are purchased at the front desk.
* **Information:** [Fort McHenry Visitor Center Info](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/visitorcenter.htm) | [Star Fort Fees](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/fees.htm)

### Fort McHenry Junior Ranger Program & Educational Youth Camps

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image2.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Fort McHenry Junior Ranger Activity Booklet and Badge">

Youth visitors can complete activity booklets onsite or online to earn an official Junior Ranger badge and certificate.

* **War of 1812 Fife & Drum Camp:** An annual summer day camp for youth ages 11 and up teaching early American military fife and drum instrumentation.
* **Program Details:** [Download Junior Ranger Booklet (PDF)](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/upload/FOMC-2017-Version-Jr-Ranger-508-reduced-size-for-web.pdf) | [Fort McHenry Youth Activities](https://www.nps.gov/fomc/planyourvisit/justforkids.htm)

### Star-Spangled Banner Flag House

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image10.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Star-Spangled Banner Flag House in Baltimore">

A National Historic Landmark built in 1793, this museum preserves the home and workshop of Mary Young Pickersgill, who hand-sewed the massive 30-by-42-foot flag that flew over Fort McHenry during the 1814 bombardment.

* **Address:** [844 E Pratt St, Baltimore, MD 21202](https://maps.app.goo.gl/FlagHouse)
* **Website:** [Star-Spangled Banner Flag House Museum](http://www.flaghouse.org/)

---

### Pride of Baltimore II (Tall Ship)

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image9.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Pride of Baltimore II Tall Ship">

A reproduction of a 19th-century Baltimore Clipper schooner that serves as Maryland's goodwill ambassador. Public deck tours and educational programming provide a firsthand look at traditional tall ship seamanship and early American maritime history.

* **Phone:** [(410) 539-1151](tel:+14105391151)
* **Tours & Schedules:** [Pride of Baltimore II Come Aboard](https://pride2.org/come-aboard/)

## Religious & Cultural Landmarks

### Lovely Lane United Methodist Church & Museum

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image11.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Lovely Lane UMC Church and Museum in Baltimore">

Recognized as the "Mother Church of American Methodism." In December 1784, delegates met at the original Lovely Lane Meeting House during the famous Christmas Conference to organize the Methodist Episcopal Church in America and elect Francis Asbury as its first bishop.

* **Address:** [2200 St. Paul Street, Baltimore, MD 21218](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LovelyLaneUMC)
* **Website:** [Lovely Lane UMC History & Beliefs](http://lovelylane.net/home/our-beliefs/)

## Waterfront Neighborhoods & Harbor Attractions

### Harborplace & Inner Harbor Amphitheater

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image6.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Harborplace Waterfront Pavilions and Inner Harbor">

Positioned along Pratt and Light Streets, Harborplace features waterfront pavilions surrounding the Harborplace Amphitheater, serving as a hub for community events, seasonal live performances, and harbourfront strolls.

* **Website:** [Harborplace Attractions & Events](https://www.harborplace.com/attractions/)

---

### Federal Hill Historic District

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image5.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Federal Hill Park overlooking Baltimore Inner Harbor">

A historic neighborhood framed by Federal Hill Park, offering panoramic views of the downtown skyline. Features the 19th-century Cross Street Market, independent galleries, and proximity to cultural landmarks like the American Visionary Art Museum and Baltimore Museum of Industry.

* **Neighborhood Guide:** [Federal Hill Baltimore Neighborhood Guide](https://baltimore.org/neighborhoods/federal-hill)

---

### Fell's Point Historic Waterfront District

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image8.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Historic Cobblestone Streets of Fell's Point">

Established around 1763 along the Patapsco River's Northwest Branch, Fell's Point retains its 18th-century maritime charm with cobblestone streets, municipal markethouses, antique shops, seafood dining, and historic waterfront pubs.

* **Directory:** [Fell's Point Places & Attractions](https://fellspoint.com/places/)

---

### Harbor East District

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/baltimore/media/media/image7.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Harbor East Waterfront Skyline in Baltimore">

A modern revitalization of Baltimore's 19th-century industrial warehouse waterfront, Harbor East is now a bustling urban center featuring high-end dining, retail boutiques, and luxury hotels.

* **Events Directory:** [Harbor East Events & Shopping](https://www.harboreast.com/events/)
