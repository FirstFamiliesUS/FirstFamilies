# Carroll County, Maryland

Formed in 1837 from parts of Baltimore and Frederick counties, Carroll County lies in the rolling hills of north-central Maryland along the Pennsylvania border. The area holds a pivotal place in American religious history as the cradle of American Methodism, alongside a rich tradition of agricultural, colonial, and 19th-century heritage.

---

## Historic & Religious Sites

### Strawbridge Shrine

<img src="{{ '/events/maryland/carroll/media/media/image1.gif' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Strawbridge Shrine Historic Farmhouse in New Windsor, Maryland">

The Strawbridge Shrine features the original home (circa 1760) of Robert and Elizabeth Strawbridge. In this modest farmhouse, Methodism in America was first established as Robert Strawbridge traveled the circuit to preach and plant the seeds of a new denomination. The site includes historic farm structures and a Visitors Center.
