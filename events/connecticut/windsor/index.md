# Windsor, Connecticut

## Overview
Settled in 1633, Windsor holds the distinction of being the first English settlement in Connecticut. Founded by pioneers arriving from Plymouth and the Massachusetts Bay Colony, Windsor served as a vital agricultural, trade, and ecclesiastical hub during the early colonial era in the Connecticut River Valley.

---

## Genealogy & Heritage Organizations

### Descendants of the Founders of Ancient Windsor (DFAW)
The Descendants of the Founders of Ancient Windsor honors the early settlers who established the town prior to the end of 1641, preserving their historical records, family lineages, and ongoing cultural impact.

<img src="{{ '/events/connecticut/windsor/media/media/image4.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Descendants of the Founders of Ancient Windsor Banner">




* **The Founders Monument:** The original Founders List was commemorated on May 30, 1930, when the Sons and Daughters of the Pilgrims erected the Founders Monument on Palisado Green (site of the first meeting house).
* **300th Anniversary Legacy:** May 30, 1930, marked the tricentennial of the arrival of the *Mary and John* off Nantasket, as well as the founding of Windsor's First Church prior to sailing from Plymouth, England.
* **Official Website:** [Descendants of the Founders of Ancient Windsor - Founders List](https://dfaw.org/founders-list/)
