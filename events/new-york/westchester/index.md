# Westchester County, New York

Located directly north of New York City along the Hudson River, Westchester County played a vital role in colonial New Netherland, provincial New York, and the American Revolution. Rich in Dutch architecture, Revolutionary history, and historic landmarks, Westchester remains an essential region for Hudson Valley historical and genealogical research.

---

## Historic Landmarks & Places of Worship

### Old Dutch Church of Sleepy Hollow

<img src="{{ '/events/new-york/westchester/media/media/image1.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Old Dutch Church of Sleepy Hollow in Westchester County, New York">

Constructed in the 17th century along Albany Post Road (U.S. Route 9), the Old Dutch Church of Sleepy Hollow is the second-oldest extant church and the 15th-oldest surviving building in New York State. Designated a National Historic Landmark in 1961 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1966, the 17th-century stone church and its historic three-acre churchyard gained worldwide renown through Washington Irving's 1820 short story *"The Legend of Sleepy Hollow"*. 

Maintained continuously by the Reformed Church of the Tarrytowns, the historic building still hosts regular Sunday summer services and special holiday gatherings.

* **Address:** [42 N. Broadway, Tarrytown, NY 10591](https://maps.app.goo.gl/OldDutchChurchSleepyHollow)
* **Phone:** [(914) 631-4497](tel:+19146314497)
* **Worship Schedule:** Sunday services held at 10:00 a.m. from mid-June through Labor Day, as well as special occasions such as Christmas Eve.
* **History & Information:** [Old Dutch Church of Sleepy Hollow (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Dutch_Church_of_Sleepy_Hollow)
