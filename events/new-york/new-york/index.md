# New York, New York

New York state and city historical research is rooted in colonial Dutch foundations, early English provincial history, and the Revolutionary era. Numerous heritage and lineage organizations preserve the genealogical records, family histories, and cultural legacies of early New Netherland and colonial New York settlers.

---

## Lineage & Heritage Societies

### Holland Society of New York

<img src="{{ '/events/new-york/new-york/media/media/image1.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Holland Society of New York Emblem">

Founded in 1885, the Holland Society of New York collects and preserves documentation relating to the history and genealogy of New Netherland and early Dutch settlements in North America.

* **Membership Eligibility:** Open to direct descendants of a Dutch native or Dutch resident who lived in New Netherland or the American colonies prior to or during the year **1675**.
* **Website:** [Holland Society Membership & Ancestor Registration](https://hollandsociety.org/join/)

---

### Society of the First Families of New York

<img src="{{ '/events/new-york/new-york/media/media/image2.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Society of the First Families of New York Crest">

Dedicated to recognizing and honoring the early ancestors who built the foundation of New York from its early colonial beginnings through the end of the American Revolutionary War.

* **Membership Eligibility:** Open to individuals at least 18 years of age who prove direct lineal descent from an ancestor of the Founding Families of the State of New York who resided within colonial New York (or its present borders) on or before **November 24, 1783** (Evacuation Day).

---

### Society of Daughters of Holland Dames

<img src="{{ '/events/north-carolina/piedmont-triad/media/media/image3.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Society of Daughters of Holland Dames Seal">

Established in 1895, the Society of Daughters of Holland Dames preserves the memory and genealogy of Dutch colonial women and early settlers of New Netherland.

* **Membership Eligibility:** Open to women at least 18 years of age who prove lineal descent through any of the following avenues:
  1. Lineal descent from a person born in the Netherlands or in New Netherland of Dutch parentage prior to the Treaty of Westminster (**1674**); OR
  2. Lineal descent from an ancestor who resided in New Netherland prior to the Treaty of Westminster (**1674**).
* **Website:** [Daughters of Holland Dames Ancestor Eligibility](https://hollanddames.org/ancestors/)

---

### Dutch Colonial Society

<img src="{{ '/events/new-york/new-york/media/media/image4.png' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Dutch Colonial Society Insignia">

Focuses on commemorating the contributions of Dutch settlers and individuals who rendered vital service to Dutch colonial culture and governance in early America.

* **Membership Eligibility:**
  * Proven direct descent from a Dutch settler born in the Netherlands who immigrated to any settlement in what is now the United States no later than **April 19, 1775**; OR
  * Proven direct descent from non-Dutch ancestors who resided in New Netherland prior to the Treaty of Westminster (**1674**) or settled in North America no later than **April 19, 1775**, AND who rendered significant business, cultural, military, religious, or political service to Dutch heritage.
* **Website:** [Dutch Colonial Society Ancestor Directory](http://www.dutchcolonialsociety.org/ancestors.htm)
