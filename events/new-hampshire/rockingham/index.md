# Rockingham County, New Hampshire

As one of the original four counties established in New Hampshire in 1769, Rockingham County holds a foundational place in New England history. Centered around early seaport communities like Portsmouth and Revolutionary hubs like Exeter—which served as New Hampshire's primary capital during the War for Independence—the region is home to essential historic archives, Revolutionary war sites, and museum collections.
