# Cincinnati, Ohio

Situated along the Ohio River, Cincinnati—known as the "Queen City"—holds a prominent place in American westward expansion, river trade, and 19th-century immigration. From colonial-era heritage organizations and deep-rooted genealogical archives to historic abolitionist landmarks and living history, Hamilton County offers an abundance of resources for family history researchers and preservation enthusiasts.

---

## Lineage & Genealogical Societies

### Hamilton County Genealogical Society (HCGS)

An active chapter of the Ohio Genealogical Society dedicated to providing educational programs, preserving regional records, and maintaining extensive pioneer databases.

* **Sponsored Lineage Societies:**
  * **First Families:** Honors direct descendants of pioneers who settled in Hamilton County prior to January 1, 1821.
  * **Settlers and Builders:** Honors direct descendants of pioneers who settled in Hamilton County between January 1, 1821 and December 31, 1860.
  * **Century Families:** Honors direct descendants of residents who settled between January 1, 1861 and 100 years ago.
  * **Civil War Soldiers:** Honors direct or collateral descendants of Civil War soldiers and certain civilian groups (1861–1865).
* **Mailing Address:** [P.O. Box 15865, Cincinnati, OH 45215](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HamiltonCountyGS)
* **Phone:** [(513) 956-7078](tel:+15139567078)
* **Website:** [Hamilton County Genealogical Society](https://hcgsohio.org/)

## Historic & Preservation Sites

### The Betts House

<img src="{{ '/events/ohio/cincinnati/media/media/image1.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="The Betts House in Cincinnati, Ohio">

Built in 1804, The Betts House is Cincinnati's oldest surviving brick home. Owned and operated as a historic house museum by The National Society of The Colonial Dames of America in the State of Ohio (NSCDA-Ohio), it offers exhibits exploring early 19th-century domestic life, construction history, and regional preservation.

* **Address:** [416 Clark Street, Cincinnati, OH 45203](https://maps.app.goo.gl/416ClarkSt)
* **Operating Organization:** [The National Society of The Colonial Dames of America in the State of Ohio](https://nscda-oh.org/)
* **Website:** [The Betts House](https://www.bettshousecincinnati.org/)

---

### Harriet Beecher Stowe House

A key historic landmark in Cincinnati's Walnut Hills neighborhood, serving as the 1830s residence of author Harriet Beecher Stowe and her family. The house explores the abolitionist movement, the Underground Railroad network along the Ohio River, and civil rights history across two major periods of significance (1840s and 1940s).

* **Address:** [2950 Gilbert Avenue, Cincinnati, OH 45206](https://maps.app.goo.gl/2950GilbertAve)
* **Website:** [Harriet Beecher Stowe House](https://www.stowehousecincy.org/)

---

### Heritage Village Museum

A living history museum located within Sharon Woods featuring thirteen restored 19th-century buildings. The museum presents historical reenactments, educational programs, and seasonal heritage events depicting 19th-century life in Southwestern Ohio.

* **Address:** [11500 Lebanon Road, Sharonville, OH 45241](https://maps.app.goo.gl/11500LebanonRd)
* **Website:** [Heritage Village Museum](https://heritagevillagecincinnati.org/)
