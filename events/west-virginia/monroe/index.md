## Key Destinations & Landmarks

### [Cook's Old Mill](http://www.cooksoldmill.com/text/genealogy-vcook-bio.html)

<img src="{{ '/events/west-virginia/monroe/media/media/image1.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Cook's Old Mill">

Cook's Old Mill is a mid-1800s gristmill located on Route 122, just ¼ mile west of Greenville, West Virginia. It is a privately owned, park-like venue for tourists, history buffs, Cook descendants, and mill enthusiasts. The mill serves as an anchor point on the Farm Heritage Road, one of Monroe County's Scenic Byways.

> **National Register of Historic Places Description:**
> 
> Cook's Mill was built in 1857 on the original foundation and site of an earlier mill constructed in approximately 1796. The property surrounding this mill includes a dam, mill pond, tail race, and stream across 3½ acres. The mill was an important gathering place for the community. Construction elements include massive hand-hewn, mortise-and-tenon posts and beams.
