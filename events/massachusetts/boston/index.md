# Boston, Massachusetts

As the cradle of the American Revolution, Boston holds a central position in American history. From colonial sea trade and early resistance movements to revolutionary battles and historic preservation, Boston offers an unmatched network of national parks, historical sites, and educational resources.

---

## National Parks & Historic Landmarks

### Boston National Historical Park

<img src="{{ '/events/massachusetts/boston/media/media/image2.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Boston National Historical Park">

Spanning 43 acres across Downtown Boston, Charlestown, and South Boston, this national historical park encompasses key sites along the historic Freedom Trail. The park maintains active visitor centers at [Faneuil Hall](https://www.nps.gov/bost/historyculture/fh.htm) and the [Charlestown Navy Yard](https://www.nps.gov/bost/historyculture/cny.htm).

* **Visitor Services:** Offers free maps, historical exhibits, and scheduled [Ranger-Guided Talks and Tours](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm).
* **Website:** [Boston National Historical Park Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/bost/planyourvisit/index.htm)

---

### Bunker Hill Monument & Museum

<img src="{{ '/events/massachusetts/boston/media/media/image3.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Bunker Hill Monument and Grounds">

Commemorating the June 17, 1775 Battle of Bunker Hill—one of the earliest major battles of the American Revolutionary War—the site includes the granite monument, lodge, and museum managed by the National Park Service.

* **Museum & Facilities:** The [Bunker Hill Museum](https://www.nps.gov/places/bunker-hill-museum.htm) features historical exhibits, staff informational services, and public restrooms.
* **Virtual Access:** Experience panoramic views from the monument via [360-degree Live Webcams](https://www.nps.gov/bost/learn/views-of-the-revolution-360-monument-webcams.htm).
* **Visitor Info:** Review site requirements and operating guidelines under [Know Before You Go](https://www.nps.gov/bost/bost-know-before-you-go.htm).
* **Website:** [Bunker Hill Monument History & Culture](https://www.nps.gov/bost/learn/historyculture/bhm.htm)

## Coastal & Island Heritage

### Boston Harbor Islands National Recreation Area

<img src="{{ '/events/massachusetts/boston/media/media/image1.jpeg' | relative_url }}" class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm d-block mx-auto mb-4" alt="Boston Harbor Islands Coastal View">

A park partnership of 34 islands and peninsulas that protects coastal natural ecosystems and preserves thousands of years of Indigenous, colonial, military, and maritime history in Boston Harbor.

* **Website:** [Boston Harbor Islands National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/boha/learn/kidsyouth/beajuniorranger.htm)
